Electrical distribution audit and optimisation

An independent diagnosis of your electrical installations to make operation more reliable, prepare a change or reduce consumption.

The challenge

A network that has grown in stages accumulates gaps: unknown power margins, protections not reviewed, incomplete documentation. An audit gives an objective view before an extension, a refurbishment or a connection request.

What we do

  • Power balance and capacity margins
  • Analysis of the distribution architecture and protections
  • Review of documentation: diagrams, cables, calculation notes
  • Identification of weak points and operating risks
  • Ways to optimise electricity consumption
  • Prioritised and costed recommendations

Our approach

  1. Scoping: scope, issues and available documents
  2. Analysis of diagrams, loads and existing documentation
  3. Check calculations: power balance, short circuits, protections
  4. Site visit and discussions with operations if necessary
  5. Report: findings, risks, prioritised recommendations

Deliverables

  • Summary and detailed audit report
  • Power balance and margins
  • List of gaps and ranked risks
  • Ways to optimise consumption
  • Action roadmap and cost estimate

Standards and references

The audit refers to French electrical regulations, the applicable IEC standards and the requirements specific to your site.

Frequently asked questions

What is an electrical distribution audit?

It is an independent diagnosis of the architecture, protections and documentation of an installation, with prioritised recommendations.

Is an audit useful before an extension?

Yes: it identifies power margins and points to correct before the extension is specified.

Is a site visit needed?

A visit is recommended when documentation is incomplete; part of the audit can be done remotely.
