Electrical studies HV/LV

Design and sizing of high and low voltage electrical networks, from feasibility studies to detailed calculations.

The challenge

A poorly sized electrical network is expensive: extra switchgear costs, unavailability, unsuitable protections. Studies secure the choices before purchase and construction, for a new substation, an extension or a refurbishment.

What we do

  • Electrical calculations and HV, MV and LV distribution architecture
  • Relay protection plans
  • Pulsed loads and safety networks
  • Cable management and cable databases
  • Feasibility studies and optimisation of electricity consumption
  • Tools: ETAP, CANECO BT

Our approach

  1. Data collection: existing diagrams, loads, operating constraints
  2. Network modelling (ETAP for high and medium voltage, CANECO BT for low voltage)
  3. Calculations: load distribution, short circuits, voltage drops, selectivity
  4. Protection plan and relay settings
  5. Summary, costed recommendations and preparation of the specifications

Deliverables

  • Calculation note and assumptions
  • Annotated single-line diagram
  • Relay protection plan
  • Power balance and capacity margins
  • Recommendations and compared options

Standards and references

The studies rely on French electrical regulations and on the international IEC standards applicable to components and tests, with the specific requirements of nuclear and research facilities where necessary.

Example assignment

66 kV network upgrade of a fusion research facility (2026): electrical requirements according to the existing transformers, integration studies, phasing relative to the live network. See projects

Detailed areas of expertise

Relay protection plan · Short circuits and selectivity · Pulsed loads · ETAP studies

Frequently asked questions

What types of network do you carry out studies for?

High, medium and low voltage networks, including pulsed loads and safety networks, for industrial, research or nuclear facilities.

Which tools do you use?

ETAP for network studies, CANECO BT for low voltage, and cable databases.

Can we limit ourselves to a feasibility study?

Yes. It lets you compare options and estimate costs and schedule before detailed design.
