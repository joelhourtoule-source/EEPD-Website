Technical specifications for electrical networks and substations

Specifications for new networks, refurbishments or capacity upgrades, with scoping of costs, schedule and interfaces.

The challenge

A clear specification sets the scope, limits price gaps between suppliers and reduces rework on site. It must be consistent with the existing installation, feasible on the civil works side and verifiable at acceptance.

What we do

  • Specification of power electrical components
  • Power requirements and upstream connections
  • Market studies and comparison of solutions
  • Cost estimates, schedule and phasing with civil works
  • Preparation of testing and commissioning

Our approach

  1. Scoping of power requirements and upstream connections
  2. Analysis of the existing installation and interfaces
  3. Market study and comparison of solutions
  4. Drafting of technical specifications and test requirements
  5. Cost estimates, schedule and phasing with civil works

Deliverables

  • Technical specifications for components and substations
  • Comparison of solutions and suppliers
  • Cost estimate and schedule
  • Acceptance and commissioning test requirements
  • Management of interfaces with other work packages

Standards and references

The specifications cite the IEC standards relevant to the manufacture and testing of power equipment, as well as applicable French regulations.

Example assignment

Specifications for a new HV/LV substation (2025): power requirements, 22 kV upstream connections, choice of equipment consistent with the existing substations, transformer sizing. See projects
