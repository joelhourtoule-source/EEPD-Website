Nuclear emergency distribution: the principles

Separation, segregation, diesel generators, UPS: principles of safety-related electrical distribution.

By Joël Hourtoule · 31 October 2026

The electrical distribution of a nuclear or research facility relies on specific design rules, such as RCC‑E, to guarantee the supply of important systems in all circumstances.

Principles

  • Separation and segregation of supply routes
  • Emergency sources: qualified diesel generators, UPS, battery-backed DC distribution
  • Operation and maintenance based on RAMI and FMEA (AMDEC) analyses

Why separation is essential

Separating and segregating supply routes limits common-cause failures: a fault on one route must not leave the important functions supplied by the other without power.

Operation and maintenance

The reliability of an emergency supply depends as much on its design as on its operation: periodic tests, preventive maintenance, availability of spare parts. RAMI and FMEA (AMDEC) analyses make it possible to organise this maintenance from the design phase.
