I had the opportunity to work with Joël for nearly 18 years, first as a colleague, then directly within his team.

Joël is without doubt one of the best managers I have worked with. He has strong technical expertise, great professional rigour and a real ability to step back and find solutions, even in the most complex situations.

But what makes him particularly strong is also his way of managing. He knows how to trust his team members, giving them autonomy while remaining available and attentive. He knows how to create a working environment where everyone can take initiative, develop their skills and progress.
