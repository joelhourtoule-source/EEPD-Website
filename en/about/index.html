Joël Hourtoule, senior electrical distribution consultant

Former ITER electrical power distribution section leader, specialist in nuclear research facilities.

EEPD Consulting is the trading name of Joël Hourtoule’s electrical engineering consulting activity. Master’s degree in engineering from Polytech Marseille (1992), BTS in power electronics (1989). Electrical safety authorisation for high and low voltage (NF C18‑510), valid until 2027. Languages: French (native), English (C1), Spanish (A2). CIGRE member since 2007.
