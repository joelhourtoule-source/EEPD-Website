Substation communication based on IEC 61850

Specification, engineering and testing of HV and MV substation communication based on IEC 61850, from protection to supervision.

Why this study

The IEC 61850 standard lets a substation’s protection, automation and supervision communicate in a common language. Well specified, it reduces cabling and makes the substation easier to extend; poorly specified, it creates interoperability problems between manufacturers, found late, during testing.

Substation communication architecture

What we deliver

  • Definition of the substation communication architecture: station, bay and process levels, Ethernet network topology
  • Specification of the devices and of the exchanges: MMS and GOOSE messages, signal list
  • Configuration engineering with SCL files (SSD, ICD, SCD, CID)
  • Review of manufacturer documents and of interoperability between devices of different brands
  • Preparation and follow-up of factory and site tests
  • Support for commissioning and feedback

Engineering: the SCL configuration files

Input data required

  • Substation single-line diagram
  • List of protection and control devices and their manufacturers
  • List of signals, commands and interlocks
  • Operation and availability requirements
  • Cybersecurity requirements of the operator

Deliverables

  • Communication architecture specification
  • List of signals and exchanges between devices
  • SCL engineering rules
  • Test plan and acceptance criteria
  • Review comments on manufacturer documents

Standards and references

The IEC 61850 series defines the architecture, the data model and SCL configuration; the IEC 62351 series addresses the cybersecurity of these communications.

Frequently asked questions

What does IEC 61850 change compared with a conventional link?

Exchanges between devices go over an Ethernet network instead of point-to-point wiring. Protection devices can exchange fast signals (GOOSE messages) and supervision reads data (MMS) from a common model.

Can devices from different manufacturers be mixed?

That is the purpose of the standard, but interoperability has to be checked: it is prepared at specification stage and confirmed during factory tests.

Do you work on existing substations?

Yes: review of the communication architecture and recommendations for upgrades.
