Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) and lightning protection

EMC rules for harsh environments (electric and magnetic fields, harmonics from power electronics) and lightning protection of nuclear facilities.

Why this study

In an installation dense in power electronics, instrumentation and cabling, disturbances travel through cables, through the earth and by radiation. Direct or nearby lightning adds overvoltages that the equipment must withstand. EMC rules and the lightning protection system are defined at design stage: correcting them after installation is expensive.

Sources, coupling and mitigation

Standards and references

Lightning protection relies on the IEC 62305 series; EMC on the requirements of your installation and the IEC 61000 series.

Frequently asked questions

Why deal with EMC at design stage?

Cable routing and separation rules cost little to apply at the start and a lot to correct once the cable trays are installed.

Is lightning protection of a nuclear facility different?

The principles of the IEC 62305 series are close to those of an industrial facility; safety and segregation requirements add constraints to the choice and location of the devices.

Do you work on existing installations?

Yes: review of the existing arrangements and recommendations, on site or remotely.
