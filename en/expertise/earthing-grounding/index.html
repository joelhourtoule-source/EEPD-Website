Earthing/grounding and neutral systems

HV neutral grounding policy, EMC rules and LV neutral distribution in a TN‑S system for nuclear facilities.

Why this study

Earthing sets how the network behaves in a fault: fault current, overvoltages, touch voltages. It also conditions EMC. The HV neutral system and the earthing topology are chosen together, early in the project, because they influence the protection plan and the whole installation.

Frequently asked questions

What is a TN‑S system?

In a TN‑S system, the neutral is earthed at the source; the neutral conductor and the protective conductor (PE) stay separate throughout the installation.

Why mesh the bonding conductors?

A meshed network provides many low-impedance paths: it limits potential differences between structures and equipment, which also helps EMC.

Does the HV neutral system affect protection?

Yes: the grounding method determines the earth-fault current, and therefore the protection settings.
