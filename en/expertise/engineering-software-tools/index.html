Software and tools for electrical studies

The tools used for network studies, sizing, protection settings and project scheduling. Remote work, over the network, on your licences.

Why this study

A study result is only as good as the model and the assumptions. Mastering the tools means we can take over your site’s model, check it and extend it, rather than start from scratch.

Remote work, on your licences

All activities on these software tools are carried out remotely, over the network, with access to the client’s licences. No installation is needed on our side; access follows your organisation’s security rules.

What we deliver

  • Network simulation: load flow, short-circuit, selectivity with ETAP and DIgSILENT PowerFactory
  • Installation sizing with CANECO BT and CANECO HT
  • HV protection relay settings: ABB, SEL, Siemens, Schneider Electric
  • Drawings and diagrams in AutoCAD
  • Project scheduling with Microsoft Project and Primavera P6

Input data required

  • Single-line diagram and equipment data
  • Existing models, if your site has any
  • References of your protection relays
  • Secure remote access to your environment and licences
  • Delivery requirements: file formats, mandated tools

Deliverables

  • Documented network model
  • Calculation notes with assumptions and results
  • Protection settings tables
  • Drawings and schedules in your formats

Standards and references

Calculations follow the IEC and NF standards applicable to your installation, including the IEC 60909 series for short-circuits.

Frequently asked questions

How do you work on our licences?

All activities on these software tools are carried out remotely, over the network, with access to the client’s licences. We do not need to install software on our side: access follows your organisation’s security rules.

Can you take over an existing model?

Yes: we check the model and its assumptions, then update it before using it.

Do you work with tools imposed by the client?

Yes, within the software listed here; otherwise we agree on an exchange format.

Which short-circuit calculations do you use?

Calculations follow the applicable IEC standards, for example IEC 60909 for short-circuits.
