Electrical network studies in ETAP

A digital model of your network to test scenarios before carrying them out.

Why this study

A network model makes it possible to assess an extension, a refurbishment or a degraded mode before any intervention. ETAP is software for modelling and analysing electrical networks, used for this type of study.

What we deliver

  • Building the model from single-line diagrams and equipment data
  • Load flow and voltage profiles
  • Short-circuit calculations
  • Protection coordination
  • Operating scenarios and degraded modes

Studies carried out with the model

Load flow

It calculates voltages, currents and losses in all operating configurations, and spots overloaded feeders or excessive voltage drops before an extension.

Short-circuits

Short-circuit current calculation, according to IEC 60909, checks the breaking capacity and withstand of switchgear and cables.

Protection coordination

The model is used to plot relay setting curves and check selectivity between upstream and downstream protection.

Scenarios and degraded modes

Loss of a transformer, emergency supply, reconfiguration: the model lets you test them without touching the installation.

Approach

  1. Collect single-line diagrams and equipment data
  2. Build the model and validate it on known cases
  3. Define operating scenarios with you
  4. Run the calculations and analyse the results
  5. Document assumptions, propose recommendations and variants

Remote work, on your licences

Studies are carried out remotely, over the network, with access to your ETAP licences: we install nothing on our side. See the Software and tools page.

Input data required

  • Single-line diagrams
  • Manufacturer data for transformers, cables and machines
  • Load profiles and operating configurations
  • Requirements of the grid operator or the facility operator

Deliverables

  • Documented ETAP model
  • Calculation notes with assumptions
  • Results per scenario
  • Recommendations and variants

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide the ETAP model?

The model and calculation notes can be delivered as agreed with you.

What about low voltage?

Low-voltage studies are carried out with CANECO BT.

Do you work on our ETAP licences?

Yes. All activities are carried out remotely, over the network, with access to the client’s licences, following your organisation’s security rules.

Can you take over an existing ETAP model?

Yes: we check the model and its assumptions, then update it before using it.
