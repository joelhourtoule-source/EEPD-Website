Short-circuit calculations and selectivity studies

Checking that switchgear and protections withstand fault currents, before and after every network change.

Why this study

Short-circuit currents determine the breaking capacity of switchgear, the withstand of busbars and cables, and the protection settings. An extension or a new connection changes them: recalculating avoids discovering undersizing in operation.

What we deliver

  • Calculation of maximum and minimum short-circuit currents at key points
  • Verification of breaking capacity and of thermal and dynamic withstand
  • Selectivity studies between upstream and downstream protections
  • Analysis of the impact of an extension or a new connection
  • Recommendations for modifying or replacing equipment

Input data required

  • Single-line diagram and characteristics of the sources
  • Impedances of transformers and cables
  • Contribution of motors and rotating machines
  • Operating configurations to be studied

Deliverables

  • Short-circuit calculation note
  • Switchgear verification table
  • Selectivity curves and recommended settings
  • List of equipment to be reinforced or replaced

Frequently asked questions

Why recalculate after an extension?

Adding sources, loads or transformers changes the fault currents and may exceed the capacity of the existing switchgear.

Which tools do you use for these calculations?

Mainly ETAP for high and medium voltage and CANECO BT for low voltage.
