Projects and references in electrical distribution

Recent assignments

66 kV HV network upgrade

Jan–Jul 2026
Upgrade of the 66 kV network of a fusion research facility: electrical requirements based on the existing transformers, market study, integration studies, phasing against the live network, cost and schedule estimates. Study approved; upgrade included in the 2027–2030 budget plan.

Specifications for a new HV/LV substation

Jan–Jul 2025
Power needs, 22 kV upstream connections, equipment consistent with existing substations, low-voltage studies and transformer sizing, cost estimates, schedule aligned with civil works, preparation of testing and commissioning. The specifications were used for a tender awarded in 2026.

SF6 circuit breaker strategy

Mar–May 2026
European F-gas Regulation (EU) 2024/573, which provides a phased SF6 phase-out between 2026 and 2032 depending on voltage level: study of alternatives, discussions with major grid operators including RTE, possible derogations, price comparison, strategy for future HV extensions.
