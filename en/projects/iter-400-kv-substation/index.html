ITER: commissioning of the 400 kV substation and HV networks

For the ITER fusion project, Joël Hourtoule led the Electrical Distribution section from 2007 to 2022. This case study covers the 400 kV substation, the entry point of the site’s power supply, and the associated HV networks.

ITER · 2007–2022

Context

For the ITER fusion project, Joël Hourtoule led the Electrical Distribution section from 2007 to 2022. This case study covers the 400 kV substation, the entry point of the site’s power supply, and the associated HV networks.

Challenges

  • Supply the whole site from the 400 kV incoming lines, at all voltage levels
  • Make protection and supervision devices from several manufacturers communicate, based on IEC 61850
  • Commission on schedule an installation interfaced with many other work packages

Our work

  • 400 kV substation with 7 bays and associated HV networks: design, procurement, installation, commissioning
  • More than 40 feeders at 66 kV and 80 at 22 kV
  • Substation communication based on IEC 61850, presented in an IEEE publication (2012)
  • Commissioning in 2017–2019, on schedule and within budget
  • Transition to operation: maintenance strategy based on RAMI and FMECA analyses

Principle of the approach

  1. Specify the electrical architecture and the expected performance
  2. Procure the equipment and follow factory tests
  3. Install, then test on site: equipment, functions, system
  4. Commission and hand over to the operation team

Outcome

Commissioning of the 400 kV substation and associated HV networks in 2017–2019, on schedule and within budget.

Skills involved

IEC 61850 communication · Testing and commissioning · ITER case study
