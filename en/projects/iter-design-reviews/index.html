Challenges

  • Bring an independent view on electrical design choices
  • Make sure findings lead to tracked actions
  • Draw on knowledge of the project, from design to operation

Our work

  • Participation in design reviews as an external electrical expert
  • Findings incorporated into the project’s action plans
  • Support from the experience of ITER electrical distribution lead (2007–2022)

How a design review runs

  1. Read the design documents before the meeting
  2. Take part in the review and ask the electrical expert’s questions
  3. Write comments, ranked by importance
  4. Follow their inclusion in the project action plan

What an electrical expert examines

  • Consistency of the distribution architecture and voltage levels
  • Protection plan and selectivity
  • Train separation and emergency power supplies
  • Electromagnetic compatibility and earthing
  • Operability and maintainability of the installation

This approach applies to any project with a significant electrical distribution, not only to large research projects.

Skills involved

Project reviews · ITER case study · Protection plan

Frequently asked questions

When should an external expert be involved?

Ideally before the design is frozen: comments then cost little to incorporate. A review is still useful later, before procurement or before commissioning.

What does the project receive after the review?

Written comments, ranked by importance, which the team incorporates into its action plan.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, document reviews are done remotely; attendance at meetings can be by video call or on site.
