Upgrading a 66 kV network

From January to July 2026, an assignment to upgrade the 66 kV HV network of a fusion research facility.

Context

From January to July 2026, an assignment to upgrade the 66 kV HV network of a fusion research facility.

Challenges

  • Defining requirements compatible with the existing transformers
  • Integrating the new equipment (available space, installation)
  • Phasing the works against the live network

Our work

  • Electrical requirements established from the characteristics of the existing transformers
  • Market study of equipment
  • Integration studies: space and installation
  • Preparation of project management, with phasing against the live network
  • Cost and schedule estimates

Approach

  1. Collect the characteristics of the existing transformers and switchgear
  2. Define electrical requirements compatible with the existing equipment: voltage levels, short-circuit currents, interfaces with protection
  3. Study the market for available equipment and compare it
  4. Study integration: available footprint, access, installation conditions
  5. Prepare work phasing to limit the impact on the network in service
  6. Estimate costs and schedule, then present the study for approval

Points to watch

  • Compatibility with existing equipment: an overly ambitious requirement forces changes to neighbouring equipment too
  • Space and access: the available room drives the choice of equipment as much as its performance
  • Outages and restoration: every intervention on a network in service is prepared with a recovery plan
  • SF6 regulation: the choice of HV switchgear must take it into account, see our article on alternatives
  • Testing and commissioning: to be planned from the moment requirements are defined

This assignment ran from January to July 2026. The duration of a study of this type depends on the size of the network and the number of items of equipment involved.

Outcome

  • Study approved
  • Upgrade included in the 2027–2030 budget plan

Skills involved

Technical specifications · Short-circuit and selectivity studies · Industry and large sites sector

Frequently asked questions

Why carry out a study before buying the equipment?

The study sets the requirements, checks integration on the site and costs the project. It reduces the gaps found during construction, when they cost the most.

How can outages on a network in service be limited?

By preparing work phasing with the operator: order of interventions, temporary supplies, outage durations and conditions for returning to the normal configuration.

What does the cost and schedule estimate cover?

Equipment, installation, studies and tests, and the sequence of tasks. Amounts specific to a client are not published.
