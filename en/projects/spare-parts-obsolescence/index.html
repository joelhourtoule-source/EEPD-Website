Spare parts and obsolescence strategy

Independent consultant assignment: define spare parts management and the obsolescence strategy of an electrical installation, for more than 1,000 references. It builds on the experience gained leading the ITER operation and maintenance team.

Independent consultant

Context

Independent consultant assignment: define spare parts management and the obsolescence strategy of an electrical installation, for more than 1,000 references. It builds on the experience gained leading the ITER operation and maintenance team.

Challenges

  • Know which parts to keep in stock, and in what quantity, to meet availability targets
  • Avoid shutting down equipment whose part is no longer manufactured
  • Track more than 1,000 references in a structured way

Our work

  • Definition of a spare parts database
  • Stock management based on the FMECA analysis: parts for the most critical equipment are handled first
  • Anticipation of obsolescence through a standardisation analysis over a multiyear programme
  • Definition of the spare parts and obsolescence strategy

Principle of the approach

  1. List the equipment and the associated references
  2. Rank equipment by criticality using the FMECA analysis
  3. Set the stock policy according to this criticality
  4. Monitor obsolescence and standardise over several years

ITER reference

On the ITER project, spare parts and component obsolescence management was part of the work of the operation and maintenance team. We provided technical assistance to set up the tools needed for follow-up.
