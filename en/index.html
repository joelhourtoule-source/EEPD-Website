Frequently asked questions

What kind of assignments do you carry out?

HV/LV electrical studies, technical specifications, project reviews, expertise on technical solutions and electrical distribution to RCC‑E.

Do you work anywhere in France and internationally?

Yes. As most tasks are carried out remotely, EEPD Consulting works anywhere in France, Europe and worldwide, in French or English. On-site visits can be agreed with the client when needed (task kick-off, specific activities).

What is your experience with nuclear facilities?

Joël Hourtoule led ITER’s electrical power distribution section from 2007 to 2022 and was an engineer, then head of electrical studies, at CEA (Tore Supra).

Which standards do you master?

French electrical regulations, international manufacturing and testing standards (IEC) and nuclear design rules (RCC‑E).

Is EEPD Consulting related to EPDs (environmental product declarations)?

No. EEPD Consulting is an electrical engineering consultancy: studies, specifications and expertise in HV/LV power distribution. It does not produce environmental product declarations.

How do we start an assignment?

Contact us by email or phone describing your project. We then define the scope and approach.
