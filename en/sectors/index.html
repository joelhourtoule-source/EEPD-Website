Sectors we work in

Research and nuclear, industry and large sites: the contexts where our experience is most useful.

Electrical distribution for research and nuclear

From the 400 kV grid to emergency power supplies: the experience of ITER and Tore Supra at the service of your facilities.

Electrical distribution for industry and large sites

HV/LV substations, capacity extensions and network refurbishments: expert insight on projects where electricity is critical.

Two contexts, one requirement

Whether a research facility or a large industrial site, the electrical distribution must stay available, fit into an existing site and evolve without interrupting operation. Only the applicable rules change: qualification and train separation for nuclear, operation and cost requirements for industry.

What both sectors have in common

  • Continuity of service and emergency power supplies
  • Integration of new equipment on a site in operation
  • Work phasing and outage management
  • Testing, commissioning and operation and maintenance

Frequently asked questions

Which sector matches my project?

If your facility is subject to safety or qualification requirements, see the research and nuclear sector. For an industrial site, or a substation or network to renovate, see the industry and large sites sector. If in doubt, describe your needs.

Do you work anywhere in France and internationally?

Yes. As most tasks are carried out remotely, EEPD Consulting works anywhere in France, Europe and worldwide, in French or English. Software-based studies are done remotely, on your licences; on-site visits can be agreed with the client when needed.
