Electrical distribution for industry and large sites

HV/LV substations, capacity extensions and network refurbishments: expert insight on projects where electricity is critical.

The challenges

On an industrial site or a large facility, electrical distribution determines continuity of service. Load changes, extensions and ageing installations require the architecture, protections and power margins to be reassessed regularly.

What we bring

  • Specifications for new or refurbished HV/LV substations
  • Network extensions and capacity increases
  • Protection plan, short-circuits and selectivity
  • Distribution audit and optimisation of electricity consumption
  • Project review and coordination of contributors
  • Support for factory inspections and acceptance tests

Specifications for a new HV/LV substation (2025) and upgrade of a 66 kV network (2026) for a research facility: the same requirements as a large industrial site in terms of continuity of service, integration and phasing.
